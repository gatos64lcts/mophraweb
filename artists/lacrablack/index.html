About

Born September 17, 2002 Jeremi Márquez (LACRABLACK) is a Venezuelan DJ and producer born in Mérida and based in Bogotá, Colombia. His music is rooted in sound exploration, where rhythm, bass weight, and texture take precedence over genre boundaries or fixed classifications.

Moving through drum and bass, garage, dubstep, house, and trap, his work treats these styles not as destinations, but as mutable frameworks—tools that bend, collide, and evolve. Each track emerges from open listening and intuitive construction, allowing influences from any context to inform structure, mood, and energy.

Rather than positioning himself within a single scene, LACRABLACK operates from a fluid, hybrid sonic space shaped by global electronic movements. His work as both DJ and producer is driven by continuous experimentation, sound deconstruction, and the ongoing search for a personal language—one that remains in flux and grows alongside the music itself.
